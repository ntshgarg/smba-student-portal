# SMBA academy handover

Use this checklist with the head coach before entering the full academy roster.

## Secure setup

- Platform owner confirms recovery email, authenticator and offline backup codes.
- Platform owner opens the one-time secure head-coach setup.
- Head coach verifies their recovery email, creates their password and PIN, connects an authenticator,
  and stores backup codes away from the phone.
- Head coach signs out and completes one fresh password + authenticator login.

## Small live pilot

Run the first acceptance pass with one junior coach and two or three consenting test players:

1. Submit and approve player and junior-coach registrations.
2. Complete player assessment, session assignment and fee-plan assignment through Player Onboarding.
3. Create one session and verify it in calendar, schedules and both relevant dashboards.
4. Record player attendance and staff roll call; correct one deliberate recording mistake.
5. Record one registration payment and one monthly payment, then verify both player and coach records.
6. Publish one announcement and one monthly report and verify player visibility.
7. Test password recovery for a player and the protected recovery path for the head coach.
8. Check the principal workflows on one real phone, one tablet and one desktop browser.

Do not add the full roster until every step above has passed and the first production snapshot or
Turso recovery point has been confirmed.

## Weekly coach routine

- Review Player Onboarding for intermediate states.
- Confirm sessions and attendance are complete.
- Reconcile recorded offline payments against receipts.
- Publish announcements and reports deliberately; do not share account credentials.
- Report unexpected redirects, missing records or duplicate operations before retrying repeatedly.

## Monthly owner routine

- Review failed production-health runs and Resend delivery failures.
- Review active sessions in Account Security and log out unfamiliar devices.
- Confirm recovery emails and offline authenticator backup codes remain available.
- Create and verify an encrypted logical database snapshot.
