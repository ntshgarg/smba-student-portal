SMBA FINANCIALS PHASE 3 — FROZEN IMPLEMENTATION RECORD
=======================================================

Status: Implemented and validated
Document date: 8 August 2026
Architecture version: Financials Phase 3


1. PURPOSE
==========

Phase 3 completes Financials as a coach-maintained operational record system.
It adds three read-only records views, private on-demand documents and an
explicit Fee Plan ending workflow. It does not introduce a payment gateway,
checkout, online collection, expenses, payroll or formal accounting.

The player experience remains unchanged. Financial state never controls
training, attendance, scheduling, reports, account access or player
development.


2. ARCHITECTURAL INVARIANTS
===========================

- UUIDs remain canonical identities and foreign keys.
- Charges, Payments, allocations, Refunds, Concessions and Adjustments remain
  immutable ledger facts corrected only through explicit lifecycle actions.
- Financial records are never hard-deleted.
- Every balance, status, register total, export and document is derived through
  shared ledger calculators. Views do not perform independent calculations.
- Reports and documents represent current ledger truth at generation time.
  Phase 3 deliberately has no historical as-of or period-close snapshot model.
- Documents and CSV exports are generated on demand, are never persisted and
  never mutate the ledger.
- Coach authorization is enforced at both route and service boundaries.
- Internal notes, fingerprints, database UUIDs and private audit metadata are
  excluded from coach documents and exports unless explicitly part of the
  operational record contract.


3. INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE
===========================

Financials now has three complementary destinations:

  Overview / player ledger
      -> current operational status and player-level actions

  Quick Record Payment
      -> repeated receipt-entry workflow

  Financial Records
      -> Fee Register
      -> Collections Day Book
      -> Activity History

Financial Records is a work surface rather than a dashboard. It contains no
charts, trends, KPIs, accounting jargon or new navigation system.


4. FEE REGISTER
===============

The Fee Register supports Monthly and Registration views. It provides:

- player name and Academy ID;
- active or archived scope;
- fee reference and due date;
- original billed amount;
- credit and debit adjustments;
- amount received and current outstanding amount;
- derived financial status;
- current summary totals;
- search by player, Academy ID or fee reference;
- status and player-scope filters;
- stable pagination;
- on-demand CSV export; and
- a private player statement download.

A missing monthly Charge is presented as Not prepared when an applicable Fee
Plan exists and Setup required when it does not. The register never stores or
mutates a separate balance or status.


5. COLLECTIONS DAY BOOK
=======================

The Collections Day Book is a dated record of receipt and Refund events. It
shows:

- event date and immutable human reference;
- player and Academy ID;
- method;
- affected fee references;
- amount and lifecycle;
- gross receipts, Refunds, net collections and method totals; and
- an authenticated receipt PDF for recorded Payment rows.

Coach-selected date ranges are limited to 366 days. Reversed entries are
hidden by default and may be included explicitly. CSV generation is streamed,
private/no-store, formula-neutralized and never persisted.


6. ACTIVITY HISTORY
===================

Activity History translates financial audit events into plain operational
language. It supports search, event type, coach and date filters, stable
pagination and an on-demand CSV export.

The UI does not expose raw JSON, payload fingerprints, database IDs or other
implementation metadata. Audit events remain immutable and are read through a
dedicated presentation contract.


7. PRIVATE FINANCIAL DOCUMENTS
==============================

Phase 3 adds two coach-only PDF documents:

- Receipt: generated for one Payment and its current Refund/reversal state.
- Player Fee Statement: generated from the player's complete current ledger.

Both documents:

- are created only after coach authorization;
- use private, no-store responses and safe filenames;
- are generated from current shared ledger projections;
- support multi-page output;
- omit private notes and internal identifiers;
- persist no file and create no database write; and
- fail with non-enumerating responses when a record is unavailable.


8. FEE PLAN ENDING AND RESTART
==============================

An active Fee Plan may be ended through an explicit coach action containing:

- the final fee month;
- a required reason;
- the expected record revision; and
- an idempotency key.

Ending a plan is transactional and audited exactly once. It changes only the
agreement's effective end and lifecycle. Existing Charges, receipts,
Adjustments and documents remain historical facts. Ending is rejected if an
issued monthly Charge already exists after the selected final month.

Restarting financial tracking creates a new effective-dated Fee Plan. The new
plan cannot overlap any historical plan for that player. An ended plan is not
reopened or rewritten.


9. QUERY AND STORAGE CONTRACTS
==============================

Phase 3 exposes purpose-built read models:

- FinanceRegisterRow
- FinanceDayBookEvent
- FinanceActivityItem
- ReceiptDocument
- StatementDocument

Query contracts validate filters, date ranges, status values, page sizes and
cursors at the service boundary. Database indexes support Charge register,
Payment date/lifecycle, Refund date/lifecycle and audit type/time reads.
Migration 0013 adds indexes only and changes no financial facts.


10. AUTHORIZATION AND PRIVACY
=============================

- Financial Records, exports, receipts, statements and Fee Plan lifecycle
  actions are coach-only.
- Player and anonymous requests are rejected before financial data is loaded.
- Private downloads are owner-scoped to the authenticated coach workspace and
  are never served from public assets.
- Player-facing Financials remains the existing restrained, read-only Phase 2
  experience.

The production authentication and managed-persistence work identified in the
principal readiness audit remains a deployment prerequisite. Phase 3 does not
replace or weaken that requirement.


11. INTENTIONALLY DEFERRED
==========================

Phase 3 does not include:

- a payment gateway, checkout, payment links or webhooks;
- automatic bank reconciliation or online settlement state;
- expenses, payroll, profit-and-loss or cash-flow accounting;
- GST, tax invoices or statutory accounting reports;
- period close, historical as-of statements or immutable document snapshots;
- automated reminders by WhatsApp, SMS or email;
- family billing accounts, wallets, credits or unapplied funds;
- charts, collection-pressure metrics or financial hero KPIs; or
- changes to the Player Portal financial experience.

Further financial capabilities require a separate approved milestone based on
observed academy operations.
