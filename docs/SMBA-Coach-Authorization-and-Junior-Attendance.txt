SMBA — Coach Authorization & Junior Coach Attendance
Frozen implementation contract

Status

APPROVED AND IMPLEMENTED

Purpose

Separate privileged academy administration from junior-coach access while giving
junior coaches a deliberately small, self-service attendance experience.

Scope

- Introduce explicit coach access levels:
  - head_admin
  - junior_coach
- Provision Sathiya Moorthy as the head administrator.
- Permit only a head administrator to access or mutate academy operations,
  including Members, Sessions, Calendar, player Attendance, Reports and
  Financials.
- Give each junior coach an authenticated Coach Workspace containing only:
  - a personal welcome surface; and
  - their own read-only staff-attendance record.
- Give the head administrator one staff-attendance register for recording daily
  Present, Absent or cleared/unrecorded outcomes for junior coaches.
- Calculate staff-attendance summaries from persisted daily facts.

Authorization invariants

- An account role of coach is not, by itself, administrative authorization.
- Every privileged page, action and externally reachable service boundary must
  require head_admin access and fail closed.
- A junior coach can read only their own attendance. They cannot read another
  coach's attendance or any player, schedule, report, member or financial data.
- Junior coaches cannot mutate staff attendance.
- The head administrator may read and record attendance for junior coaches but
  does not appear as a row in the junior-coach register.
- Authorization is enforced on the server. Hidden navigation is not a security
  boundary.

Staff-attendance invariants

- One junior coach has at most one attendance outcome per academy date.
- Dates use Asia/Kolkata date-only semantics.
- Attendance cannot be recorded before the coach's joined date or after the
  current academy date.
- Present and Absent are explicit persisted facts; a cleared date is unrecorded.
- Attendance percentage is Present / (Present + Absent). Unrecorded and
  unavailable dates do not affect the denominator.
- Staff attendance is independent of player session schedules, rosters,
  payroll and finance.
- Account and attendance identifiers use immutable UUID relationships rather
  than Academy IDs.

Out of scope

- Public or self-service coach registration.
- Assistant-coach access to academy administration.
- Coach shifts, working-hour schedules, leave management or payroll.
- Session-linked coach attendance.
- Head-coach attendance tracking.
- Delegated permissions or configurable role policies.

Validation contract

- Head administrators can open every existing coach workflow and mutation.
- Junior coaches are redirected or denied for every head-only route and action.
- Direct requests cannot bypass role restrictions.
- A junior coach sees only their own attendance and cannot mutate it.
- Duplicate coach/date attendance writes remain impossible.
- Joined-date, future-date and Asia/Kolkata boundary behavior is deterministic.
- Present, Absent and cleared changes survive reload and produce the expected
  summary.
- TypeScript, lint, unit/integration tests and production build pass.

Change policy

This contract is frozen. New junior-coach capabilities require a separate
authorization milestone and must not be inferred from the coach account role.
