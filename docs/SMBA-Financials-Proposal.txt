SMBA FINANCIALS V1 — FROZEN IMPLEMENTATION SPECIFICATION
========================================================

Status: Approved for implementation
Prepared for: SMBA Coach Workspace and Player Journal
Document date: 8 August 2026
Architecture version: Financials V1


1. PURPOSE AND PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY
=================================

Financials is a first-class operational feature of the Coach Workspace. It
helps the academy answer three questions:

1. What has the player been charged?
2. What money has the academy received?
3. What still requires attention?

Financials is not a Paid / Not Paid flag and is not an accounting dashboard.
It is a small, auditable ledger built from independent financial facts:

  Fee Agreement -> Charge -> Payment
                          -> Adjustment

The coach sees simple operational language. The player sees a quiet,
informational fee record. Training and development remain the centre of the
product.


1.1 Architectural invariants
----------------------------

- All financial calculations are deterministic and derived exclusively from
  persisted ledger facts.
- No balance, outstanding amount or payment status is stored in the database.
- Charges describe what is owed.
- Payments describe what was received for one charge.
- Adjustments explain an explicit change to a charge's effective amount.
- Financial records are never hard-deleted. Charges are voided, payments are
  reversed, and adjustments are reversed through new auditable facts.
- Every financial mutation is authorized, validated, transactional,
  idempotent where retry is possible, and represented by an audit event.
- All money is stored as integer paise. Floating-point arithmetic is forbidden
  for persisted values and balance calculations.
- Account UUID is the immutable owner key. Academy ID is searchable display
  data and is never a foreign key.
- Fee references are generated once, stored as opaque immutable identifiers,
  and never used to derive chronology or business logic.
- Financial status never affects training access, attendance, scheduling,
  monthly reports, account access or report generation.
- The Financials activation is a one-time, irreversible academy cutover. It
  cannot be toggled off after activation; corrections happen through the
  ledger and audit trail.


1.2 Phase 1 goals
-----------------

Coach goals:

- See which players are paid, pending, partially paid, overdue or require
  setup.
- Track the one-time registration fee and monthly training fees.
- Prepare a month's fees explicitly and safely.
- Record an offline payment quickly, including partial payment.
- Search by player name, Academy ID or fee reference.
- Reverse an incorrect payment without deleting history.
- Apply a justified credit, debit or historical settlement.
- Review one player's complete fee record.

Player goals:

- See current fee status without payments dominating the Player Journal.
- Understand the current amount due, due date and fee reference.
- Review registration, monthly-charge and payment history.
- See factual, calm language rather than collection-oriented messaging.


1.3 Explicitly deferred
-----------------------

Phase 1 does not include:

- Online payments or payment-gateway webhooks.
- Multi-charge payment allocation.
- Family or parent billing accounts.
- Fee-product or price-catalogue database tables.
- Automated proration.
- Automated fee generation jobs.
- Refund workflows.
- Downloadable receipts or invoices.
- Tax/GST accounting.
- Bank reconciliation.
- WhatsApp, SMS or email payment reminders.
- Discounts, waivers or scholarships as dedicated product workflows.
- Accounting-system integrations.

These are extension points, not incomplete Phase 1 work.


2. FIXED BUSINESS RULES
=======================

2.1 Money and pricing
---------------------

- Currency is INR.
- Monetary columns store positive integer paise.
- Registration fee is INR 1,000 (100,000 paise).
- Canonical monthly prices come from one checked-in typed configuration shared
  with the public fee guide.
- An issued charge snapshots its amount. Later pricing changes never rewrite a
  historical charge.
- An active Fee Agreement snapshots the player's agreed monthly fee and Academy
  Plan.


2.2 Registration fee
--------------------

- Registration-fee charging begins only after Financials activation.
- A newly approved player receives at most one active registration charge.
- The charge is due three calendar days after approval.
- Existing players are reconciled during setup as pending, settled before the
  portal, or unresolved.
- Settled before portal is represented by a legacy-settlement adjustment. A
  fake historical payment must not be created.
- An unresolved registration remains Setup required and visible in the coach's
  attention queue even after monthly training Charges are paid. It leaves that
  coach-attention state only after explicit registration reconciliation.
  Registration reconciliation is a separate fact from the player's actual
  ledger status: a real pending, partially paid or overdue monthly obligation
  must still show its amount and due date in player/detail views.


2.3 Monthly fee
---------------

- The coach explicitly selects Prepare <Month> fees.
- The operation is idempotent and creates only missing eligible charges.
- One player can have at most one active monthly-training charge per billing
  month.
- The normal due date is the fifth of the billing month.
- Monthly billing starts from the first effective session assignment.
- If the first assignment begins on or before the fifth, the first charge is
  due on the fifth.
- If the first assignment begins after the fifth, the first charge is due three
  calendar days after the assignment date.
- The first month is charged in full. Phase 1 has no proration.
- A monthly charge requires both an applicable Fee Agreement and a scheduled
  session assignment whose effective dates and session-series window overlap
  the selected billing month. A historical assignment that ended earlier does
  not make later months billable.
- A player without an effective assignment or Fee Agreement is not charged and
  appears as Setup required or Awaiting assignment.
- An otherwise eligible player whose selected month has not been prepared is
  shown as Month not prepared and remains in the coach's attention view.
- Coach attention and outstanding totals include every issued monthly arrear
  up to the selected billing month. Paying the current month never hides an
  older unpaid Charge. The Received summary may remain scoped to the selected
  billing period.
- Reading a page never creates charges.


2.4 Payments
------------

- A Phase 1 payment belongs directly to one charge.
- A payment may be partial.
- A payment must be greater than zero and cannot exceed the charge's current
  outstanding amount.
- Supported methods are Cash, UPI, Bank transfer, Card, Cheque and Other.
- External reference and internal note are optional.
- Retrying a payment with the same idempotency key returns the original result
  and does not create another payment.
- Optimistic charge revision prevents two coaches from settling the same
  remaining balance concurrently.
- If one concurrent operation settles the charge first, the other receives a
  deterministic conflict rather than creating an overpayment.


2.5 Corrections
---------------

- Recorded payments are not edited.
- An incorrect payment is reversed with a required reason and actor.
- A successful payment reversal creates exactly one corresponding
  payment_reversed audit event. Retries must not create duplicate reversal
  facts or duplicate audit events.
- An issued charge is not silently edited.
- A charge may be voided only with a reason and only when it has no active
  recorded payment.
- Manual credit decreases the effective amount owed.
- Manual debit increases the effective amount owed.
- Legacy settlement clears only the current outstanding amount being
  reconciled.
- Adjustments require a reason and may themselves be reversed, preserving the
  complete history.
- Reversing a debit calculates the post-reversal effective amount with the
  same zero floor as the ledger. It is blocked only when recorded Payments
  would exceed that floored amount.


3. DOMAIN MODEL
===============

Financials V1 uses exactly five finance tables:

1. fee_agreements
2. financial_charges
3. payments
4. charge_adjustments
5. financial_audit_events

There is no BillingAccount, BillingAccountMembership, FeeProduct, FeePrice or
PaymentAllocation table in Phase 1.


3.1 Fee Agreement
-----------------

Domain name: FeeAgreement
Coach-facing name: Fee plan
Storage table: fee_agreements

Purpose:

- Describes the commercial terms agreed for one player over an effective date
  range.
- Snapshots Academy Plan, level, batch, agreed monthly fee and due-day policy.
- Does not create attendance eligibility or session assignments.

Required fields:

- id: immutable UUID/text primary key.
- player_account_id: immutable Account UUID foreign key.
- academy_plan: weekday-3-day, weekday-4-day, weekday-5-day, or
  weekend-standard.
- level: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Adult.
- batch: Weekday or Weekend.
- agreed_monthly_fee_paise: positive integer.
- currency: INR.
- monthly_due_day: integer 1-28; Phase 1 default 5.
- effective_from: date-only key.
- effective_to: nullable date-only key.
- status: active, paused, or ended.
- record_revision: optimistic concurrency integer.
- created_by_account_id and updated_by_account_id.
- created_at and updated_at.

Constraints:

- At most one active Fee Agreement per player.
- effective_to cannot precede effective_from.
- Ending or replacing an agreement never changes historical charges.


3.2 Charge
----------

Domain name: Charge
Storage table: financial_charges

Purpose:

- Records an issued registration or monthly-training obligation.
- Owns the immutable public-facing fee reference.
- Holds the amount snapshot, description, billing period and due date.

Required fields:

- id: immutable UUID/text primary key.
- fee_reference: stored opaque reference matching SMBA-XXXXXXXX.
- player_account_id: Account UUID foreign key.
- fee_agreement_id: nullable Fee Agreement foreign key.
- type: registration or monthly_training.
- billing_period: null for registration; YYYY-MM for monthly training.
- description: immutable issued description.
- original_amount_paise: positive integer snapshot.
- currency: INR.
- due_date: date-only key.
- lifecycle: issued or void.
- record_revision: optimistic concurrency integer.
- issued_by_account_id and issued_at.
- voided_by_account_id, voided_at and void_reason when void.

Constraints:

- fee_reference is unique case-insensitively.
- At most one issued registration charge per player.
- At most one issued monthly-training charge per player and billing period.
- A void charge must record actor, timestamp and non-empty reason.
- A void charge remains queryable and auditable.

Fee reference rules:

- Format: SMBA-XXXXXXXX using an unambiguous uppercase alphabet.
- Generated once with cryptographically secure randomness.
- Does not encode date, sequence, player identity or database identity.
- Used for display and search only.


3.3 Payment
-----------

Domain name: Payment
Storage table: payments

Purpose:

- Records money received for one Charge.
- Supports a recorded or reversed lifecycle.

Required fields:

- id: immutable UUID/text primary key.
- charge_id: Charge foreign key.
- player_account_id: Account UUID foreign key.
- amount_paise: positive integer.
- currency: INR.
- received_on: date-only key.
- method: cash, upi, bank_transfer, card, cheque, or other.
- external_reference: nullable, length-limited text.
- internal_note: nullable, length-limited text; coach-only.
- lifecycle: recorded or reversed.
- idempotency_key: unique retry key.
- recorded_by_account_id and recorded_at.
- reversed_by_account_id, reversed_at and reversal_reason when reversed.

Constraints:

- A reversed payment must retain its original value and record actor, timestamp
  and non-empty reason.
- Reversal never removes the original payment.
- player_account_id must match the charge owner.
- Player-facing queries never expose internal_note.


3.4 Charge Adjustment
---------------------

Domain name: Adjustment
Storage table: charge_adjustments

Purpose:

- Applies an explicit credit, debit or historical settlement to one Charge.
- Preserves why an effective amount differs from its issued amount.

Required fields:

- id: immutable UUID/text primary key.
- charge_id: Charge foreign key.
- kind: manual_credit, manual_debit, or legacy_settlement.
- amount_paise: positive integer.
- reason: required text.
- created_by_account_id and created_at.
- reversed_by_account_id, reversed_at and reversal_reason when reversed.

Constraints:

- Active credits and legacy settlements reduce the effective amount.
- Active debits increase the effective amount.
- Reversed adjustments remain in history but no longer affect calculations.
- An adjustment reversal is an explicit authorized operation and audit event.


3.5 Financial Audit Event
-------------------------

Domain name: FinancialAuditEvent
Storage table: financial_audit_events

Purpose:

- Records who performed each material finance mutation, what changed and when.
- Provides the unique one-time Financials activation marker.

Required fields:

- id: immutable UUID/text primary key.
- actor_account_id: coach Account UUID.
- event_type.
- entity_type: academy, fee_agreement, charge, payment, adjustment, or player.
- entity_id.
- idempotency_key: nullable unique retry key.
- metadata: versionable JSON text containing non-sensitive event context.
- occurred_at.

Required event types:

- finance_activated.
- fee_agreement_created.
- fee_agreement_replaced.
- fee_agreement_paused.
- fee_agreement_ended.
- charge_issued.
- charge_voided.
- monthly_fees_prepared.
- payment_recorded.
- payment_reversed.
- adjustment_created.
- adjustment_reversed.
- historical_reconciled.

Audit rules:

- Audit events are append-only.
- One logical mutation creates one corresponding event.
- Idempotent retries never create duplicate events.
- Payment reversal must create exactly one payment_reversed event.
- There can be exactly one finance_activated event for the academy.
- Metadata must not contain secrets or unnecessary private payment details.


4. DERIVED LEDGER CALCULATIONS
==============================

For one issued Charge:

  active_debits
    = sum(manual_debit adjustments not reversed)

  active_credits
    = sum(manual_credit and legacy_settlement adjustments not reversed)

  effective_amount
    = max(0, original_amount + active_debits - active_credits)

  received
    = sum(recorded payments not reversed)

  outstanding
    = max(0, effective_amount - received)

Derived visible status:

- void: charge lifecycle is void.
- paid: outstanding is zero and settlement is represented by payments or
  normal adjustments.
- settled_before_portal: outstanding is zero, a legacy settlement exists, and
  no recorded payment is being represented as historical receipt.
- partially_paid: received is greater than zero and outstanding remains.
- overdue: no active payment has been received, outstanding remains and due
  date is before the injected reference date.
- pending: outstanding remains and due date is today or later.
- not_prepared: the player is eligible for the selected month but no active
  monthly Charge exists yet.
- setup_required: the player has no applicable Fee Agreement/charge state.

Status precedence for player summaries is:

  overdue -> not_prepared -> partially_paid -> pending -> paid/settled_before_portal

The calculation is a pure domain function. It accepts a reference date rather
than reading the current clock internally.


5. FINANCE LIFECYCLES
=====================

5.1 Academy activation
----------------------

  Not active
      -> Coach reviews the one-time cutover
      -> Coach selects the first tracking month
      -> Transaction appends finance_activated
      -> Active forever

Activation is an irreversible academy-level cutover. There is no Deactivate
operation and no feature flag that deletes or hides ledger truth after
activation. A first tracking month may not be later than the current academy
month. Replaying an already completed activation preserves the irreversible
activation result rather than replacing it.


5.2 Fee Agreement lifecycle
---------------------------

  active <-> paused
  active/paused -> ended
  active -> replaced by a new effective-dated agreement

Replacing an agreement ends the old agreement and creates a new one in a
single transaction. Existing Charges continue to reference the historical
agreement snapshot. Fee Agreements begin on the first day of a billing month,
so one deterministic agreement applies to each player/month.


5.3 Charge lifecycle
--------------------

  issued -> void

Void is terminal. A replacement obligation is a new Charge with a new opaque
fee reference. Voiding never rewrites payments or adjustments. A void Charge
retains its original amount for history, while its effective amount and
outstanding amount are both presented as zero.


5.4 Payment lifecycle
---------------------

  recorded -> reversed

Reversed is terminal. A corrected receipt is a new Payment with its own
idempotency key.


5.5 Adjustment lifecycle
------------------------

  active -> reversed

Reversal records actor, timestamp and reason and removes the adjustment only
from derived calculations, never from history.


6. SERVICE AND API BOUNDARIES
=============================

Phase 1 uses two implementation-facing boundaries:

  FinanceService
  FinanceRepository

Avoid premature decomposition into catalogue, allocation or billing-account
services.


6.1 FinanceService responsibilities
-----------------------------------

Commands:

- activateFinance(trackingMonth).
- createOrReplaceFeeAgreement(...).
- setupExistingPlayerFinance(...).
- issueRegistrationChargeForApprovedPlayer(...).
- prepareMonthlyCharges(period).
- recordPayment(...).
- reversePayment(...).
- applyChargeAdjustment(...).
- reverseAdjustment(...).
- voidCharge(...).

Queries:

- getCoachFinanceDashboardSummary(period).
- getCoachFinanceWorkspace(period, filters, search, selectedPlayer).
- getPlayerFeeRecord(playerAccountId, referenceDate).
- getPlayerFinanceDashboardSummary(playerAccountId, referenceDate).

Rules:

- Command methods receive an authenticated coach actor from the server, never
  from trusted client input.
- Query methods use route-specific DTOs. Dashboard cards must not load the
  complete 100-player ledger.
- Player queries derive the owner Account UUID from the authenticated session.
- All date-sensitive calculations receive an injected reference date/instant.
- Every command validates runtime input before opening a transaction.


6.2 FinanceRepository responsibilities
--------------------------------------

- Persist the five finance entities.
- Provide set-based summaries and filtered/paginated player lists.
- Lock or revision-check mutable lifecycle rows for concurrency safety.
- Enforce idempotency and uniqueness constraints.
- Keep reads free of side-effect writes.
- Never expose internal notes through player-facing result shapes.


6.3 Mutation contract
---------------------

Every financial mutation follows this order:

1. Authenticate the session.
2. Authorize the coach role.
3. Parse and validate a shared runtime schema.
4. Begin one database transaction.
5. Re-read current ledger facts/revisions.
6. Enforce domain invariants.
7. Write the ledger fact.
8. Write exactly one matching audit event.
9. Commit.
10. Revalidate only affected coach/player routes.

Failures preserve entered form data and return a safe operation-specific error.
Database error details and private identifiers are not exposed to clients.


6.4 Concurrency and idempotency
-------------------------------

- Payment commands require a unique idempotency key and expected charge
  revision.
- Monthly preparation uses a stable period-scoped idempotency key plus database
  uniqueness for player/period.
- Reversal and adjustment commands use their own stable idempotency keys.
- If two coaches attempt to settle the final balance simultaneously, one may
  succeed. The other must receive Charge already settled or Balance changed.
- A duplicate network retry returns the first successful result without
  creating a second payment or audit event.
- Idempotent Fee Agreement and existing-player setup retries must match the
  complete original command payload; reusing a key for different business
  input returns a deterministic conflict.


7. AUTHORIZATION AND SECURITY
=============================

- Only an authenticated coach may activate Financials, configure Fee
  Agreements, prepare fees, record/reverse payments, apply/reverse adjustments
  or void charges.
- A player may read only financial records owned by their authenticated Account
  UUID.
- Never accept actorAccountId or player owner identity from an untrusted client
  when it can be derived from the session or selected authorized record.
- Coach list/detail queries must enforce coach authorization server-side.
- Player routes must be protected against IDOR even if a UUID is guessed.
- Internal notes and audit metadata are coach-only.
- Finance mutation endpoints require origin/CSRF protection consistent with the
  authenticated application.
- Input length, enum, date, month, money and idempotency constraints are
  validated at runtime and reinforced by database constraints.
- Sensitive values are excluded from logs.
- Academy-ID-only prototype authentication is not sufficient for public finance
  deployment. Production phone OTP, password or passkey authentication with
  rate limiting must be completed before Financials is publicly released.


8. COACH WORKSPACE UX
=====================

8.1 Dashboard integration
-------------------------

Place Financials after Monthly Reports and before Members.

The compact card may show:

- Outstanding amount.
- Number of players needing attention.
- A single Open Financials action.

Do not add charts, collection graphics, payment-method breakdowns or historical
trends to the dashboard card.


8.2 Financials workspace
------------------------

Primary route:

  /coach/financials

Supported URL state:

  ?period=YYYY-MM
  &view=attention|all|pending|partially_paid|overdue|paid|setup_required
  &query=<search text>
  &player=<account UUID>

The page should reveal the first useful control in the initial mobile viewport.
Recommended order:

1. Compact title and back navigation.
2. Activation/setup state when required.
3. Billing-period selector.
4. Received, outstanding and attention summary.
5. Search and status view.
6. Player rows.
7. Focused player record.

Search matches:

- Player name.
- Academy ID.
- Exact/partial fee reference.

Default ordering:

1. Overdue.
2. Partially paid.
3. Pending.
4. Setup required.
5. Paid/settled.

Within a group, order by due date then player name.


8.3 First-time activation and existing-player setup
----------------------------------------------------

Before activation, show one restrained setup surface explaining that Financials
will become the academy's permanent ledger from the selected tracking month.

After activation, existing players require a compact setup/reconciliation flow:

- Confirm Academy Plan, level and batch from enrollment defaults.
- Confirm agreed monthly fee and effective month/date.
- Classify registration fee as pending, settled before portal or unresolved.
- Save the complete setup transactionally.

Never guess ambiguous historical payments.


8.4 Prepare Month
-----------------

Preview must distinguish:

- Ready.
- Already prepared.
- Awaiting assignment.
- Setup required.

Confirmation creates only missing eligible monthly Charges. Re-running the
operation is safe and reports reused/skipped results.


8.5 Quick Record Payment
------------------------

This is the primary repeated coach workflow:

  Search player/reference -> choose outstanding charge -> enter amount -> save

Fields:

- Amount received.
- Received date.
- Method.
- Optional external reference.
- Optional internal note.

Success uses one inline confirmation without shifting surrounding controls.
Conflicts preserve form values and explain that the balance changed.


8.6 Administrative corrections
-------------------------------

Reverse payment, apply credit/debit, reverse adjustment and void charge are
progressively disclosed inside a selected financial record. They are not
primary dashboard actions.

Every correction requires a reason and a review step appropriate to its risk.


9. PLAYER PORTAL UX
===================

Place a subtle Fee record card after Monthly Reports and before Player Profile.

The card communicates at most:

- Current status.
- Current outstanding amount, if any.
- Next due date, if any.
- View fee record.

Primary route:

  /player/financials

The read-only page groups:

- Registration fee.
- Current monthly fee.
- Prior monthly fees by year/month.
- Recorded and reversed payment history.
- Fee references.

Player-facing language remains factual:

- Paid.
- Payment pending.
- Partially paid.
- Overdue.
- Settled before portal.
- Fee details are being prepared.

Do not show internal notes, audit events, coach controls, technical adjustment
terminology or collection-oriented warnings.


10. MIGRATION AND ACTIVATION
============================

10.1 Schema migration
---------------------

1. Add the five finance tables, indexes, unique constraints and CHECK
   constraints in one versioned migration.
2. Do not create Financials records from a read path or migration side effect.
3. Deploy code with Financials inactive.
4. Verify migration and rollback/restore procedures on a disposable database.
5. Authorize a coach to perform the one-time activation and select the first
   tracking month.
6. Reconcile existing players explicitly.
7. Prepare the first active month only after setup review.

Activation is a one-time irreversible cutover for the academy. There is no
supported path to return to the pre-Financials state. A database restore is an
operational disaster-recovery action, not product deactivation.


10.2 Existing-player reconciliation
------------------------------------

For each approved player:

1. Resolve current enrollment defaults.
2. Confirm the Fee Agreement and effective date.
3. Confirm registration state:
   - pending;
   - settled before portal; or
   - unresolved.
4. Issue/reuse the registration Charge.
5. Apply a legacy-settlement adjustment only when the academy confirms it was
   previously settled.
6. Never manufacture a historical Payment.
7. Record historical_reconciled in the audit trail.


10.3 Production persistence dependency
---------------------------------------

Financials must not be publicly deployed on an ephemeral or instance-local
SQLite file. Before production:

- Use a managed transactional database, or a documented single stateful Node
  deployment with an absolute persistent-volume path.
- Run migrations as a release step rather than on the first user request.
- Back up the database and test restoration.
- Add health/readiness checks and privacy-safe structured logging.
- Complete production authentication and security headers.


11. EDGE CASES AND REQUIRED BEHAVIOUR
=====================================

- Duplicate approval/retry creates at most one active registration Charge.
- Repeated Prepare Month creates no duplicate monthly Charge.
- A player approved before activation is not charged until explicit
  reconciliation.
- A player without a session assignment is not monthly billable.
- A player whose assignment or session series does not overlap the selected
  month is not monthly billable for that month.
- Fee Agreement creation and replacement are accepted only at month
  boundaries, and candidate selection cannot double-count a player/month.
- The first finance tracking month cannot be in the future.
- An ended Fee Agreement valid during a historical period remains eligible for
  that historical period.
- A later plan/price change does not alter already issued Charges.
- A partial Payment leaves the exact remaining balance.
- Reversing that Payment restores the exact balance.
- Reversing a Payment never creates or deletes another Payment.
- One Payment reversal creates exactly one audit event, including after retry.
- A Payment cannot exceed the current outstanding amount.
- Concurrent final payments cannot over-settle a Charge.
- A void Charge cannot accept new Payments.
- A void Charge presents zero effective and outstanding value while preserving
  its immutable original amount.
- A Charge with an active Payment cannot be voided until the Payment is
  explicitly reversed.
- Credits cannot produce a negative outstanding balance.
- Legacy settlement covers only current outstanding value.
- A reversed adjustment no longer affects calculations but remains visible in
  coach history.
- Date-only values remain date-only; instants are displayed in Asia/Kolkata.
- Long names and large fee references wrap without horizontal overflow.
- Financial status never blocks attendance, sessions, reports or login.


12. TEST STRATEGY
=================

12.1 Pure domain tests
----------------------

- Integer-paise effective amount, received and outstanding calculations.
- Credit, debit and legacy-settlement behavior.
- Reversed Payment and reversed Adjustment behavior.
- Every derived status around due-date boundaries.
- Asia/Kolkata/reference-date determinism.
- Opaque fee-reference validation.
- Idempotency-key validation.


12.2 Database and service tests
-------------------------------

- Migration applies to clean and existing databases.
- Foreign keys and CHECK constraints pass integrity checks.
- Finance activation can occur exactly once.
- Initial activation rejects a future tracking month.
- Activation cannot be reversed through a product operation.
- Approval issues at most one registration Charge after activation.
- Existing-player reconciliation is idempotent.
- One monthly Charge per player/period.
- Monthly preparation and preview require an overlapping assignment and
  session-series window.
- Fee Agreements start on the first of a month and cannot produce duplicate
  preparation candidates for one player/period.
- Players eligible for an unprepared month remain visible in attention counts.
- Prepare Month is repeatable and side-effect safe.
- A historical ended agreement remains usable for its valid period.
- Payment recording updates derived balance without stored status/balance.
- Partial Payment and reversal produce correct ledgers.
- Payment reversal produces exactly one audit event.
- Retried payment reversal produces no duplicate event.
- Adjustment creation/reversal produces one event per successful operation.
- Charge void rules are enforced.
- Two simultaneous final-balance payments: one succeeds; the other receives a
  deterministic conflict.
- Transaction rollback leaves neither a partial ledger fact nor orphaned audit
  event.


12.3 Authorization and security tests
-------------------------------------

- Anonymous requests cannot read or mutate Financials.
- Player A cannot read Player B's fees by changing route/query identifiers.
- A player cannot invoke coach mutations.
- Internal notes never appear in player DTOs or HTML.
- Actor identity cannot be forged through form fields.
- Forged enum, date, month, negative amount, oversized note and invalid
  idempotency inputs are rejected.
- Origin/CSRF protections apply to mutation requests.


12.4 End-to-end workflow tests
------------------------------

- Activate -> reconcile player -> prepare month -> record partial payment ->
  record final payment.
- Registration pending -> payment -> paid.
- Settled-before-portal reconciliation without fake payment.
- Quick Record Payment search by name, Academy ID and fee reference.
- Reverse a mistaken Payment and observe restored balance in coach and player
  views.
- Prepare Month remains stable after refresh/back/retry.

14. RISKS AND TRADE-OFFS
========================

Risk: Phase 1 ties one Payment directly to one Charge.
Trade-off: This keeps manual collection simple. Multi-charge allocation is a
clear Phase 2 migration rather than premature complexity.

Risk: Canonical pricing is checked-in configuration rather than database data.
Trade-off: It gives the current two-fee model one source of truth without
building a catalogue UI. Charges snapshot values, so future catalogue migration
does not rewrite history.

Risk: No automated proration.
Trade-off: Full first-month charging matches the selected academy policy and is
far easier to explain. Exceptional cases use explicit, auditable adjustments.

Risk: Explicit monthly preparation requires a coach action.
Trade-off: Phase 1 prioritizes operational transparency over invisible jobs.
The action is idempotent and shows what will happen before confirmation.

Risk: Local prototype authentication and persistence are not production-safe.
Mitigation: Financials may be built and tested locally, but public release is
blocked until production authentication and durable persistence are complete.


15. FINAL IMPLEMENTATION DECISION
=================================

Financials V1 is frozen around five ledger tables and two service boundaries.
It intentionally avoids ERP abstractions while preserving the invariants that
matter:

1. Financial truth is append-only and auditable.
2. Balances and statuses are derived, never stored.
3. Money is integer paise.
4. Charges, Payments and Adjustments remain separate facts.
5. Fee references are opaque and immutable.
6. Financials never changes training operations.
7. Activation is a one-time irreversible academy cutover.
8. Every successful Payment reversal creates exactly one audit event.

Status: APPROVED FOR IMPLEMENTATION.

Do not expand Phase 1 architecture unless implementation reveals a concrete
correctness or safety requirement. Future capability should be added from real
academy usage, not speculative complexity.
