SMBA FINANCIALS PHASE 2 — FROZEN IMPLEMENTATION RECORD
=======================================================

Status: Implemented and validated
Document date: 8 August 2026
Architecture version: Financials Phase 2


1. PURPOSE
==========

Phase 2 evolves a Payment from a one-Charge fact into one receipt that may be
allocated across one or more Charges. It adds auditable Refunds, fee
Concessions and an on-demand Collections export while preserving the quiet,
operational character of Financials.

Financials remains separate from training. Financial state never controls
attendance, scheduling, reports, account access or player development.


2. ARCHITECTURAL INVARIANTS
===========================

- UUIDs are canonical identities and foreign keys.
- Receipt and Refund references are immutable human references only.
- All money is stored as positive integer paise in INR.
- Balances and statuses are derived from persisted ledger facts and are never
  stored as mutable result columns.
- A Payment amount equals the exact sum of its immutable allocations.
- A Refund amount equals the exact sum of its immutable Refund allocations.
- Published allocations are never edited.
- A Payment is corrected by reversing the complete receipt and creating a new
  Payment. A Payment with an active Refund cannot be reversed.
- A Refund is corrected through an explicit Refund reversal.
- A Concession application creates one concession_credit Adjustment.
- A concession_credit can be reversed only through its linked Concession
  application; the generic Adjustment workflow cannot mutate it.
- Financial records are never hard-deleted.
- Every mutation is coach-authorized, transactional, idempotent, revision
  checked where concurrent balance changes are possible, and audited once.
- Financial presentation never exposes internal notes, database UUIDs,
  fingerprints, audit metadata or coach-only reasons to a player.


3. STORAGE MODEL
================

Phase 2 adds these ledger entities to the Phase 1 model:

- payment_allocations
- refunds
- refund_allocations
- concessions
- concession_applications
- finance_reference_sequences

payments remains the receipt-level fact. Its legacy charge_id is nullable and
is retained only for a clean Phase 1 migration/compatibility path. New
multi-Charge relationships use payment_allocations.

Receipt references use:

  SMBA-R-YYYY-NNNNN

Refund references use an independent yearly sequence:

  SMBA-RF-YYYY-NNNNN

Sequence allocation and the corresponding ledger write occur in the same
SQLite immediate transaction. Failed transactions consume no number.


4. PAYMENT WORKFLOW
===================

The coach's primary repeated workflow is:

  Search player
      -> enter one received amount
      -> review oldest-first suggested allocations
      -> optionally override allocations
      -> record the receipt
      -> return focus to player search

The coach may override the suggestion, but the confirmed allocation must:

- contain distinct Charges belonging to the selected player;
- reference issued Charges only;
- not exceed any current Charge balance;
- equal the complete Payment amount; and
- match the current optimistic Charge revisions.

There are no unapplied funds or wallet balances in Phase 2.


5. REFUND WORKFLOW
==================

A Refund belongs to one recorded Payment. Refund allocation suggestions undo
the newest covered Charge first, ordered deterministically by due date,
issuance time and UUID. The coach may override the suggestion.

A Refund:

- cannot exceed the remaining refundable amount;
- cannot predate its Payment;
- cannot be dated in the future;
- references only allocations belonging to its Payment; and
- changes the affected Charge balances without changing the original receipt.

Refund and Payment corrections remain explicit reversal workflows. Reversing
a Refund restores its refundable allocations; it does not delete history.


6. CONCESSIONS
==============

Concessions may be one-off or recurring, and may use a fixed paise amount or a
percentage stored in basis points.

- Percentage values are calculated from the original Charge with half-up
  rounding.
- Every Concession credit is capped at the Charge's remaining balance.
- One-off Concessions may be applied exactly once. If corrected, create a new
  one-off Concession rather than rewriting the original.
- Active recurring Concessions for one player cannot overlap.
- Recurring Concessions apply idempotently when monthly fees are prepared.
- A recurring Concession created after a fee was prepared may be applied
  explicitly to an existing in-period outstanding monthly Charge.
- Ending a recurring Concession stops future use. Existing applications and
  credits remain historical facts.
- Reversing an individual application removes only its linked credit through
  the dedicated, audited Concession workflow.

The player sees the neutral label "Fee concession" and never sees the private
reason or internal lifecycle terminology.


7. COLLECTIONS EXPORT
=====================

The coach may export current Payment and Refund events for a date range of at
most 366 days. The CSV is generated on demand, streamed, private/no-store and
never persisted.

Export fields are limited to:

- date
- event type
- receipt or Refund reference
- player name
- Academy ID
- method
- amount
- affected fee references

CSV cells are formula-neutralized. Internal notes, account UUIDs, contact
details and audit metadata are excluded. A Refund lists only the fee
references touched by that Refund's own allocations.


8. AUTHORIZATION AND PRIVACY
============================

- Coach routes and Server Actions verify an authenticated coach.
- Finance services independently enforce the same coach authorization.
- Collection exports are coach-only.
- Player Fee Record data is derived from the authenticated player's Account
  UUID and is read-only.
- Player views contain public receipt facts, allocations, Refunds and neutral
  Concession entries only.

The production authentication and managed-persistence work identified in the
principal readiness audit remains a deployment prerequisite. Phase 2 does not
weaken or replace that requirement.


9. MIGRATION
============

Migration 0012 preserves every Phase 1 Payment and:

- creates one deterministic receipt reference per existing Payment;
- creates one matching Payment allocation using the historical charge_id;
- retains the old charge_id as a nullable compatibility field;
- initializes the applicable yearly receipt sequence; and
- leaves all financial amounts, dates, methods, reversals and audit history
  unchanged.

Migration verification requires SQLite integrity, foreign-key integrity,
unique receipt references and an exact Payment-to-allocation sum match.


10. INTENTIONALLY DEFERRED
==========================

Phase 2 does not include:

- online payment gateways or webhooks;
- wallets, unapplied credits or overpayments;
- family billing accounts;
- receipt or invoice PDFs;
- GST/tax accounting;
- aging reports;
- automated reminders by WhatsApp, SMS or email;
- bank reconciliation;
- bulk payment imports;
- historical as-of exports; or
- accounting-system integrations.

These require a separately approved Phase 3 architecture and real operational
evidence.
