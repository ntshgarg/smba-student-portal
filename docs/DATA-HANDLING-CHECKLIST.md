# SMBA data-handling checklist

This is an operational checklist, not legal advice. The academy should confirm its final policy with
an appropriately qualified adviser before collecting the full student roster.

## Before collecting real records

- Record who owns the academy data and who may act as platform owner, head coach and junior coach.
- Explain to players or guardians what identity, training, attendance, report, announcement and fee
  information the portal stores and why it is needed.
- Obtain and record the academy's chosen consent or acknowledgement process, especially for minors.
- Define how a player or guardian requests access, correction, export or deletion of their records.
- Define retention periods for rejected requests, inactive members, attendance, reports, financial
  records, authentication audit events and database backups.
- Keep offline payment evidence outside the portal only where the academy's policy requires it; never
  place card, bank or authenticator secrets in free-text fields.

## Access rules

- Platform owner uses dashboard preview and narrowly scoped security support only.
- Head coach manages academy operations.
- Junior coaches receive only the workspace permissions needed for their role.
- Players see only their own records.
- Accounts are individual; credentials and authenticator backup codes are never shared.

## When a member leaves

1. End future session assignments and fee plans using the existing effective-dated workflows.
2. Reconcile any eligible mid-term refund without deleting the payment history.
3. Archive the member account instead of rewriting historical attendance, reports or finance records.
4. Apply the academy's documented retention period and record any approved export or deletion action.

## Incident records

For any suspected unauthorized access, record the discovery time, affected accounts, sessions
revoked, secrets rotated, database recovery point considered, notifications made and final outcome.
Do not copy passwords, PINs, email codes, authenticator secrets or full recovery links into the log.
