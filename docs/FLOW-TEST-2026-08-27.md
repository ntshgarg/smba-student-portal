# End-to-end flow test — 27 August 2026

A walk through the product from a genuinely empty academy, in the order a real
academy would meet it. Not an audit of the code: every observation below came
from driving the running application and reading what it put on screen.

**Build:** branch `ux-sweep-final-fixes` at `f790bcd` (PR #116), production build.
**Academy:** `prepare.ts --empty-academy` + `provision-platform-admin.ts` — one
account, `SMBA-ADMIN-0001`, and nothing else.
**Configuration:** coach TOTP **required**, so authenticator enrolment is exercised
rather than skipped. Authenticator codes are generated by the harness; the email
verification code is recovered from its challenge row, which stores
`HMAC(secret, "email-verify_email:{subject}:{challengeId}:{code}")` — every term
but the six-digit code is in the row, so a local search recovers it.

## Findings

Severity is calibrated the way this product is used: a defect that loses or
misstates a courtside register outranks the same defect on a finance screen;
money second; first-run and marketing last. "Objective" means it breaks a task,
a standard, or the product's own stated pattern — not that I would have designed
it differently.

### F1 — The day-one dashboard reports onboarding as complete

**Severity:** medium · **Confidence:** high · **Objective**
**Where:** `/coach`, Academy Onboarding card, on an academy with zero players.

The first screen a new head coach ever sees says **"Academy onboarding is
complete"** under a `CLEAR` status stamp, with `00` in all four stage counters.
Nothing has been onboarded. Nobody has registered.

The three sibling cards get this right on the same screen: Sessions says `SETUP`
/ "No training scheduled yet.", Financials says `SETUP` / "Financial records are
not set up", Members says `SETUP` / "0 enrolled members". Onboarding alone
reports the *finished* state for a condition that means *nothing has started*,
so the one card that should be pointing a new academy at its first action is the
one telling it there is nothing to do.

This is the same class as the Reschedule Attendance empty state fixed in PR #116:
a zero-data branch that reads as an outcome rather than as a beginning.

*Suggested fix:* give the card a `SETUP` state when the academy has no players
and no pending requests — "No one to onboard yet", pointing at how a family
registers — and keep "complete" for an academy that has actually cleared a
queue.

**Status: fixed** in `5f23653`. The card now has three states rather than two,
told apart by a new `onboarded` count -- `total === 0 && onboarded === 0` is an
academy that has not started, anything else with `total === 0` has finished.
Re-ran the day-one dashboard on a one-account academy: `SETUP` / "No one to
onboard yet". Re-checked after a request arrived (`1 IN PROGRESS`) and after the
queue was cleared ("Academy onboarding is complete").

### F2 — The onboarding fee field validates through the browser, not the product

**Severity:** low · **Confidence:** high · **Objective**
**Where:** the Fee Plan stage of `/coach/onboarding`, `input[name="monthlyFee"]`.

The field is `type="number" step="1" min="1"`, so a fractional, negative or zero
fee is refused — but by Chrome, in a native bubble reading *"Please enter a valid
value. The two nearest valid values are 3500 and 3501."* Measured:
`validity.valid` is false for `3500.555`, `-100` and `0`, and true for `3500`.

So the money is safe. What is inconsistent is how it says so. Every other money
input in the product refuses through `parseRupeesToPaise` and an `InlineNotice`
with `aria-invalid` — the payment desk answers `1000.555` with "Enter a valid
payment amount" beside the field. This one field speaks in the browser's voice,
on the screen that sets what a family is billed every month.

*Suggested fix:* the house treatment — `inputMode="decimal"`, the shared parser,
and the field wired to its own message.

**Status: fixed** in `b0c6ae7`, and the finding understated it twice. The
handler's own message, `aria-invalid` and focus return were already written and
simply unreachable, because native constraint validation blocks submit before
React sees it. And the `type="number"` made the field's own `3,500` placeholder
unenterable — a number input will not take the separator it was advertising.

The "shared parser" was also two parsers: `rupeesToPaise` and
`parseRupeesToPaise`, byte-identical, in two files. One is kept, beside
`formatInr`, whose doc comment already described it as its round-trip partner.

Re-ran the Fee Plan stage: `3500.555`, `-100` and `0` are each refused with
"Enter the agreed monthly fee, like 3,500", the field takes focus back, and no
native bubble appears. `3,500` is now accepted and prices correctly — 1 of 13
eligible August sessions, so ₹250 beside the ₹1,000 registration fee.

### F3 — Withdrawn. "Review announcement" does say what is missing

**This finding was wrong, and the fault was in my probe, not the product.**

I reported that pressing **Review announcement** with no destination ticked did
nothing and said nothing. Re-measured on the same screen, the product does all
four of the things I said it did not:

| | reported | actually measured |
|---|---|---|
| message | none | `Choose where the announcement should appear.` renders, visible |
| `aria-invalid` | absent | `true` — on the `fieldset`, not on the checkboxes |
| focus | not moved | moves to the fieldset, which is `aria-describedby` the message |
| dialog | blocked silently | blocked, with the reason on screen |

Three probe errors produced the false reading. I read `aria-invalid` from
`input[name="channels"]` when it is set on the group, which is the correct place
for a rule about the group rather than either box. I looked only for
`role="alert"`/`role="status"`, but the message is not a live region — it swaps
in place in the slot the field already points at, and focus moves there, so a
screen reader announces it on arrival. That is the better pattern of the two: it
is announced once, in context, rather than fired at the user from elsewhere.
And my "page text is byte-identical" check was `after.replace(before, "")`,
which finds nothing to strip when the change is in the middle of the document,
so it reported no change for a change it could not see.

Verified in the browser in both branches — no destination: dialog blocked,
message shown, group invalid, focus on the group; one destination: dialog opens.
No fix was made because none is needed.

### F4 — The authenticator gate tells the platform owner they are a coach

**Severity:** low · **Confidence:** high · **Objective**
**Where:** `/auth/two-factor/setup`, the password confirmation that precedes
enrolment, when the account is the platform owner.

The copy reads: *"This confirms that you control the coach account."* The
platform owner is not a coach — the same screen is reached by both roles and the
sentence is written for one of them. It is the owner's first interaction with
the product after provisioning.

*Suggested fix:* one conditional string, the way `/auth/pin/setup` already
branches its copy on `mandatory`.

**Status: fixed** in `f77ac18`. The tab title had the same defect and is not
mentioned above — it read "Secure coach account". The helper sentence now
follows `account.role`, which the page already resolves to gate entry; the title
is made neutral rather than conditional, since it is a noindex interstitial and
"Secure your account" is true for both roles.

Re-ran scenario 1 from a one-account database. In a single run the owner's gate
reads "This confirms that you control the platform owner account." and the head
coach's, created later in the same run, still reads "...the coach account."

## What is working well

Recorded because it is load-bearing and a careless change would undo it.

1. **The authenticator enrolment screen.** QR *and* manual key, ten recovery
   codes, copy-all and download, and the submit button held disabled with the
   reason stated in place: *"Verifying replaces this screen and the codes are
   never shown again, so this button stays locked until the box above is
   ticked."* It is the clearest consent gate in the product.

2. **Approval notices name the outcome and the next step.** Approving a junior
   coach reports *"Arun Kumar approved as SMBA-JC-6065. They can activate their
   staff account in the registration browser"* — the issued identifier and the
   condition the person needs to satisfy, in one sentence.

3. **The activation flow's failure state is honest and recoverable.** Returning
   in a different browser gives *"Use the browser where you submitted your
   registration request. / The secure browser receipt is missing, expired, or no
   longer usable."* with a "Request registration" action rather than a dead end.

4. **Pending approval names the person.** *"Approval is pending. Your coach is
   reviewing Priya Raman's request."* with a "Check again" control.

5. **Schedule conflicts are refused precisely and fast.** *"A schedule already
   uses this level, batch and time slot during the selected dates"*, returned in
   0.3 s. Creation itself lands in ~1 s with a "Schedule created" notice and a
   "Manage roster" follow-on.

6. **The junior-coach boundary holds exactly as specified.** Every head-coach
   surface — members, financials, schedules, onboarding, reports, announcements,
   player attendance — redirects a junior coach to
   `/coach?notice=head-coach-only`, and their workspace is the personal
   attendance ledger and nothing else. This matches
   `docs/SMBA-Coach-Authorization-and-Junior-Attendance.txt` line for line.

7. **The onboarding row is a genuine work surface.** Expanding a person shows a
   four-stage tracker with green checks as stages complete, a personalised next
   action ("Review Arun's staff request", "Confirm Aarav's Fee Plan"), a
   requested → approved → training start → activated → completed timeline, and
   explicit Approve/Reject controls. Every stage transition reports what happened
   *and* what is next: *"Aarav Bhat's assessment is saved. Assign the matching
   session next."*

8. **The plan and the roster cannot disagree.** Choosing a 3-day plan pre-ticks
   three weekdays; taking one away is refused with *"Choose exactly 3 attendance
   days for this Training plan"*. Since the plan is what the family is billed
   for, this is the money-relevant invariant on that screen, and it holds.

9. **Plan options cascade off the batch.** Weekday offers 3-, 4- and 5-day plans;
   Weekend offers the weekend plan alone. There is no way to pick a combination
   the academy does not sell.

10. **Financial activation is a consent gate, not a button.** A month picker plus
    *"I understand this creates SMBA's permanent financial ledger. It cannot be
    reset or switched off later; corrections remain auditable."* Same family as
    the recovery-codes gate, and the right weight for the action.

11. **Fee issuing states its own arithmetic before it commits.** *"Review first.
    Completing onboarding issues the registration fee and every applicable
    monthly fee. The joining month is prorated by eligible scheduled sessions and
    rounded once to the nearest ₹50."* Then it does exactly that: joining on
    27 August, Kabir's ₹5,000 weekend plan billed ₹1,000 for 2 of 10 eligible
    sessions; Aarav's ₹3,500 3-day plan billed ₹250 for 1 of 13. Every figure
    rounded to ₹50 as promised.

12. **Partial payment is the best-built surface in the product.** ₹500 against
    ₹1,250 outstanding shows a live projected balance, then an allocation review
    that splits the money across named charges — each with its fee reference and
    remaining amount, and the monthly charge labelled *"First monthly training
    fee · 1/13 sessions"*, so the coach can see why it is ₹250. A running
    *"ALLOCATED ₹500 of ₹500"* keeps the sum honest, and over-allocating to
    ₹750 of ₹500 disables "Record payment" rather than warning after the fact.
    Receipts are sequential — `SMBA-R-2026-00001`.

13. **Attendance marking is exactly as advertised.** Marking one player raised
    "1 unsaved change", saving raised "Attendance saved", **focus stayed on the
    Save button** rather than dropping to `<body>`, and returning to the picker
    showed the session as **"1/1 marked"** instead of the old bare "Available".
    An empty roster says *"No eligible players. Only players assigned to this
    occurrence can be recorded."* Upcoming sessions are disabled and labelled,
    so a coach cannot mark a register before the session starts.

14. **Report drafts survive a reclaimed tab.** Typing into the editor writes
    `smba-report-draft-v1:<playerId>:<month>`; reloading restores the text with
    *"Unsaved report text restored from an earlier visit. Nothing is recorded
    until you save the draft"*; Save draft and Publish both report cleanly.

15. **Login is correct for every role, and its refusals give nothing away.**
    Platform owner and head coach both pass an authenticator gate before landing
    (`/admin`, `/coach`); a head coach signing in by PIN still meets that gate; a
    junior coach goes straight through, matching the frozen contract. A wrong
    password, an unknown Academy ID, and a real player who has never activated
    all return the *same* sentence — *"Academy ID or password is incorrect. If
    this is your first visit, activate your account."* — which resists
    enumeration and still names the way forward.

16. **The monthly cycle guards a bulk money action by naming its size.** Issuing
    September goes "Review fee issue" → **"Issue 8 fees"** with a Cancel beside
    it → *"8 monthly fees issued"*. All eight charges matched their agreed fee
    exactly (₹31,500 total, due the 5th) with no proration, which is right for a
    full month. August's control is locked and reads "Fees issued", so the same
    month cannot be billed twice.

17. **The player's own dashboard is the best-composed screen in the product.**
    Next session with date, time, court and *"Arrive 15 minutes for a proper
    warm-up"*; attendance as *"12 sessions are waiting for the coach's record"*
    rather than a bare zero; reports as *"Your coach's feedback will appear here
    after your first month of training"*; the fee balance with its due date; and
    the published announcement carrying PINNED and NEW. Every card explains a
    zero state instead of showing one.

18. **The member record answers the questions the row raises.** The directory row
    says "1 active"; opening it names the session and its days — *"Beginner ·
    Weekday · 6–7 Am · Mon, Tue, Wed"* — alongside registration, approval,
    training-start, activation and onboarding dates, and it is candid about gaps
    ("PRIMARY CONTACT: Not Added", "ACCOUNT ACTIVATED: Not Completed").

- **"Schedule creation takes minutes" — wrong, three times over.** The form
  deliberately does not navigate on success; it stays and shows a notice, and

  ticked boxes. Unticking to two days is refused. The rule holds.

  product's own notice and native constraint validation does not raise one.

  instead of the group that carries it, looking only for live regions when the
  message is announced by focus moving to the field it describes, and a text

### F5 — The session picker says "Available" when there is nobody to mark

**Severity:** low · **Confidence:** high · **Objective**
**Where:** `/coach/attendance/players/record`, the day's session list.

The academy was created on a Thursday. Onboarding gave the three-day weekday
plans Mon/Tue/Wed and the weekend plans Sat/Sun, so no player trains Thursday --
correct, and not a defect. What the picker then showed was four sessions, each
stamped **AVAILABLE**:

```
6:00 am  Beginner · Weekday    Court 1   Available
7:00 am  Intermediate · Weekday Court 1  Available
8:00 am  Advanced · Weekday    Court 2   Available
9:00 am  Adult · Weekday       Court 2   Available
```

Every one of them opens to "0 players / No eligible players." The roster panel
is exact; the picker above it is not. `Available` is the same word the picker
uses for a session with thirty players and nobody marked yet, so the coach has
to open each session to learn which of the two it is.

The count is already resolved at the picker.
`components/coach/attendance/player-attendance-recorder.tsx:487-489` reads
`progress?.marked ? \`${progress.marked}/${progress.eligible} marked\` :
"Available"`, and `rosterProgressForOccurrences` returns `eligible` alongside
`marked` -- so `eligible === 0` is known and discarded. The neighbouring comment
argues, rightly, that "0/30 marked" would read as an accusation on a register
nobody has touched. That reasoning does not cover `0/0`, which is not an
untouched register but an empty one.

*Suggested fix:* one more branch -- when `progress.eligible === 0`, say so
("No players") instead of "Available". Same class as F1: a zero state wearing
the word for a non-zero one.

**Status: fixed** in `2b0a854`. Verified with all four states on screen at
once: a session with one player reads "Available" before marking and
"1/1 marked" after, the three with no roster read "No players", and the evening
sessions still read "Upcoming". Getting there needed a player assigned to a
Thursday, which the run had none of -- onboarding a seventh player onto Thursday
also closed the gap that attendance marking itself had never been exercised.

### F6 -- An onboarded junior coach was in no directory at all

**Severity:** low · **Confidence:** high · **Unclassified when filed**
**Where:** `/coach/members`.

With four junior coaches approved and six players onboarded, the Member
Directory read "6 members" and listed the six players. No coach appeared,
including the head coach. That is structural rather than a filter default:
`listApprovedPlayerRecords` is `eq(accounts.role, "player")` over an inner join
to `player_enrollments`, and staff have no row there, so the join alone excludes
them. It is then re-filtered twice more on the client.

Which left the staff roll call as the only surface carrying a junior coach's
name -- a row to tick, not a record to read. Names there are not links and there
is no staff detail anywhere, so a head coach who wanted to look a junior coach
up had nowhere to go.

Filed unclassified on purpose: an academy with a handful of coaches may not need
a staff directory, and that is a product decision rather than a defect to
declare.

**Status: fixed** in `ade9aa5`, on the decision to put staff in the existing
directory rather than build a second one. Staff are read and typed separately so
that `isAcademyMember` -- the guard the directory throws on, which stands between
a partial read and a roster that quietly drops people -- keeps meaning what it
meant. A Role filter chooses between everyone, players and coaching staff; the
three training filters are disabled in staff mode and say why, rather than
looking live and doing nothing.

Verified on a 13-person academy: Everyone 13, Coaching staff 5, Players 8, a
search for "Kavya" finds the junior coach, and staff survive a batch filter that
would otherwise have removed them. Junior coaches are listed and still cannot
open the directory; the head-coach gate is untouched.
